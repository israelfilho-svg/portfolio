How it works

From raw footage to posted

Step 1

Send the footage

Share a folder link and a short brief: the goal, a reference video you like, and the platform. I confirm scope and price before I start.

Step 2

First cut in 48–72 h

You get the edit with captions, music and sound levels done, as a private review link you can comment on.

Step 3

Revise and deliver

Two rounds of changes are included. Final files come in the right size for each platform, plus a clean version without captions.

Captions, titles and motion graphics are built from reusable templates, so once your style is set, every new video matches the last and revisions are quick.
